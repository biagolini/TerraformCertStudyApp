What's the actual difference between an IAM role and a resource-based policy?

A **resource-based policy** is attached directly to the resource (e.g. an S3 bucket policy) and says who can access that resource. A **role** is an identity a principal assumes to get temporary credentials — used when the *caller*, not the resource, needs the permissions attached.
