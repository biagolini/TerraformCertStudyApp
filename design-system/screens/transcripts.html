VPC Networking — Layered Summary

This summary was assembled by AI from the transcripts you supplied. Re-read it against the original sources before publishing.

Foundations

A VPC is an isolated network within AWS. Subnets divide it by Availability Zone; a subnet is "public" only if its route table sends 0.0.0.0/0 to an Internet Gateway.

Intermediate

Private subnets reach the internet outbound-only through a NAT Gateway placed in a public subnet. Security groups are stateful and evaluated at the ENI level; NACLs are stateless and evaluated at the subnet boundary.

Advanced

For many VPCs, prefer a Transit Gateway hub over full-mesh VPC peering — peering doesn't support transitive routing, so it becomes unmanageable past a handful of VPCs.
