A

Store new objects in S3 Intelligent-Tiering.

Comment

Intelligent-Tiering moves each object between access tiers automatically based on its own access pattern, with millisecond access in the frequent and infrequent tiers.

B

Transition all objects to S3 Glacier Deep Archive after 30 days.

Comment

Deep Archive retrievals take hours, which breaks the millisecond-access requirement when an old video becomes popular again.

C

Leave the archive access tiers of Intelligent-Tiering disabled.

Comment

The optional Archive and Deep Archive Access tiers are asynchronous; keeping them off guarantees every object stays retrievable in milliseconds.

D

Move the objects to an Amazon EBS Cold HDD (sc1) volume.

Comment

EBS volumes are block storage attached to instances; they are more expensive per GB at this scale and add operational overhead.

General explanation

Unknown or changing access patterns point to Intelligent-Tiering; predictable patterns point to explicit lifecycle transitions.
