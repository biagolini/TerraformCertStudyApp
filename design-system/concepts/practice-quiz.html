Networking

A company runs a three-tier web application across two Availability Zones. The operations team wants requests to keep being served automatically if one entire Availability Zone becomes unavailable, with no manual intervention. Which change should they make?

A Deploy the application in a single, larger Availability Zone
B Place an Application Load Balancer in front of instances spread across both Availability Zones, in an Auto Scaling group
C Take a manual snapshot of the instances every night

Why B is correct

An Application Load Balancer spanning both zones, paired with an Auto Scaling group, automatically detects unhealthy targets and reroutes traffic to the healthy zone without any manual step — meeting the "no intervention" requirement directly.
